Deal Finder Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Deal Finder is a personal trading-card research and listing-analysis application. It searches available marketplace listings, compares prices and sales evidence, and may analyze listings from an eBay seller account when that account holder authorizes access.

Information used

When connected to eBay, Deal Finder may access eBay account identifiers, active listing identifiers and details, prices, item conditions, inventory and offer data, and associated marketplace information, limited to the permissions the account holder grants. It also processes public marketplace listings and information about completed sales obtained from permitted data sources.

How information is used

Information is used to show listings, compare current asking prices with available sales evidence, estimate costs and potential resale value, and suggest pricing decisions. The initial listing-advisor workflow is intended to provide read-only recommendations; it does not automatically change eBay listings.

Service providers

Deal Finder communicates with eBay APIs and may use a third-party market research provider, CompSniper, to obtain comparable-sales data. Information sent to those providers is subject to their respective policies. Deal Finder is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay.

Storage and security

Application credentials and eBay user access tokens are intended to be handled privately on the application server and not published on this website. Listing analyses or research results may be retained by the application where needed for its features. Access tokens should not be shared publicly.

Data access and deletion

Users can revoke Deal Finder's eBay authorization through their eBay account settings. For questions about data handled by Deal Finder or to request deletion of data retained by the application, contact the application operator using the contact method below. Requests will be reviewed and handled as applicable.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Deal Finder's features or data handling change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an issue at Deal Finder Privacy Policy — GitHub Issues. Do not include access tokens, passwords, or other sensitive information in public issues.
